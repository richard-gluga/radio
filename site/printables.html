Transfer these from the parts you received

  • Trace the amplifier and RC522, including the header sockets. Put the antenna under a thin non-metal tapping area; test with the speaker and battery in position.
  • Trace the speaker mounts, display window and USB plug body. Allow both speaker moving areas to radiate.
  • Measure the pot, button and switch mounting bushings, thread lengths and rear bodies. Make a cardboard trial panel; do not drill from a nominal shaft diameter.
  • Keep the battery separate from boards, sharp edges and hot components. Allow access to inspect and unplug it.

Measure the scale square in both directions. If it is not 50 mm, correct the print scaling before using these outlines. No enclosure has yet been physically checked for this project.
