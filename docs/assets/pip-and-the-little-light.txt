Pip and the little light

Pip the kangaroo found a tiny light beside the creek.
It blinked once. Then twice.

“Are you a fallen star?” Pip whispered.

“A firefly,” said a small voice. “And I have lost my way.”

Pip held out a leaf. The firefly climbed aboard.
They followed the creek past sleeping ducks and whispering reeds.
At the old gum tree, a hundred little lights began to blink.

“Home!” said the firefly.

Pip watched the branches sparkle.
One small light had become a whole sky of friends.

“Goodnight,” Pip whispered.
And all the little lights blinked back.
